# PRD & Prototype Sprint (Module 4)

## Pick & scope with MoSCoW
- **The “Now” feature I’m scoping (name + one-line core description):** Driver Alert Notifications - Push alerts on route changes/compliance flags, can reduce reliance on SMS and keeps drivers engaged in-platform
- **My finalized Must-Haves (after overriding the AI):** Driver opens app and immediately sees the next delivery step (GPS triggers auto-advance to the next step)
Swipe right to advance to see future steps
Chat feature that lets the driver directly message coordinator, with built in quick-reply, frequent questions.
- **What I demoted from Must → Should/Won’t, and why:** Hands-free task completion: "Mark delivered"

## Generate your Simplified PRD
- **One thing my PRD makes explicit that a vague brief would have missed:** GPS is central to this proposed solution, which also airs the technical considerations earlier than normal and in a leaner way.
