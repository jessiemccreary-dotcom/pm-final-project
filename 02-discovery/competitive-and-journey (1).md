# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** Frontline Driver who relies on the mobile app throughout the day to execute assigned routes and complete deliveries efficiently.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Complete deliveries quickly, accurately, and with minimal effort while staying focused on the physical job of driving and package handling.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** "To mark a stop delivered I tap through three screens. In the rain, at a doorstep, with a package in one hand. I've started just texting my dispatcher instead."
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** SMS/Text Messaging
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** 1. Deliver package.
2. Skip delivery-completion workflow in the app.
3. Open phone messaging app.
4. Send text message to dispatcher indicating delivery was completed.
5. Dispatcher manually interprets and updates records as needed.
6. Continue route.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** Frontline driver decides to send a text message to the dispatcher instead of going through the three screens in the platform to notify and record that a package has been delivered.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** "To mark a stop delivered I tap through three screens. In the rain, at a doorstep, with a package in one hand. I've started just texting my dispatcher instead."
